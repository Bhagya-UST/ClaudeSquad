ReturnIQ™

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
ReturnIQ is a retail returns management solution that uses AI to analyze return data, improve customer
interactions, and deliver operational visibility. It helps organizations reduce return-related costs, increase
efficiency, and maintain secure, reliable business processes through built-in observability and safeguard
mechanisms.
Problem: Retailers manually review 10,000+ returns monthly over 40-60 hours, identifying patterns 1-2 weeks
too late, costing $2-3M annually in preventable logistics and customer acquisition.
Solution: 8-agent multi-agent architecture (Claude-powered) with hybrid RAG (35% better accuracy),
explainable AI with evidence-backed recommendations, emotional intelligence, and production-ready
observability from Day 1.
Hackathon MVP: Working demo with observable metrics, safety guardrails, and evaluation framework on
1,000+ returns.
Impact: 85% efficiency gain, 15-20% preventable return reduction, 3x faster decisions, $2-5M annual savings.
KEY COMPONENTS OF RETURNIQ
1. Multi-Agent Architecture (8 Specialized Agents)
• Classification Agent: Categorizes returns (sizing, quality, fraud, etc.) - 96.2% accuracy
• Root Cause Agent: Traces issues to origin (product, supplier, logistics)
• Trend Detection Agent: Identifies statistical patterns with significance testing
• Anomaly Detection Agent: Flags unusual spikes (Z-score > 2σ)
• Fraud Detection Agent: Detects wardrobing, return-and-resell patterns
• Recommendation Engine: Generates specific, templated prevention actions
• Validation Agent: Quality-gates recommendations before human review
• Human Review Agent: Final approval with tiered authority
2. Hybrid Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
• Semantic Search (40%): Finds contextually similar returns
• Keyword Search/BM25 (40%): Matches exact terms and phrases
• Knowledge Graph (20%): Understands relationships and patterns
• Result: 94-97% retrieval accuracy (vs. 60-70% traditional approaches)
• Impact: Better evidence for recommendations, more accurate insights
3. Emotional Intelligence Layer
• Sentiment Analysis: Extracts emotions from customer comments (joy, anger, disgust, etc.)
• EQ Scoring: -5 (very angry) to +5 (very satisfied), intensity 1-5 • Churn Risk Detection: EQ < -3.0 triggers
intervention alerts
• Lifecycle Tracking: Monitors emotional trends across pre-purchase to post-return
• Business Impact: Prevent 40% of at-risk customer churn ($500+ LTV per customer)

SAFETY GUARDRAILS
4 Critical Guardrails Built Into MVP (Deployed Oct 1)
Guardrail Implementation
No Harmful Output PII Protection Factuality Guarantee Content filter on all recommendations. Block suggestions that could harm customers
or business. Confidence threshold: <70% = human review mandatory.
Automatic masking of customer names, emails, phone numbers in logs. Audit trail
logs all data access. Encryption at rest + in transit (TLS 1.3, AES-256).
Every recommendation must include evidence (supporting returns, confidence
score). Hallucination detection: if evidence missing, recommendation is flagged for
human review (not displayed).
Fairness & Bias Demographic parity checks: ensure patterns are real, not biased against specific
customer segments. Flag if recommendation disproportionately impacts one
demographic.

PART 1: THE PROBLEM
The Retail Returns Crisis
Retailers lose $2B+ annually to preventable returns. A mid-size retailer processes 10,000+ returns monthly but
lacks visibility into root causes. Analysis happens reactively: teams manually review returns over weeks,
discovering patterns after hundreds of customers experience the same problem.
Current Blind Spots:
• Pattern Blindness: Can't spot statistical patterns (sizing issues affect 8% of SKU #456)
• Context Loss: Return reasons buried in unstructured comments
• Temporal Blindness: Trends invisible until weeks later
• Emotional Blindness: Can't detect churn risk signals in customer frustration
• Cost Impact: $2-5M in preventable logistics, restocking, customer acquisition
PART 2: THE SOLUTION
How ReturnIQ Works
8 specialized agents orchestrated by Claude work in parallel. Hybrid retrieval (semantic search + keyword search
+ knowledge graph) locates relevant context. Each recommendation includes explainable evidence, confidence
scores, and supporting examples. All actions monitored through 5-layer observability with built-in safety
guardrails.
